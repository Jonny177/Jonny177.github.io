About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Nanjing University, where I began my doctoral studies in 2024. My advisor is X. P. Yang. I completed my undergraduate studies at Jilin University. My research interests lie in partial differential equations and geometric analysis.

Research Interests

  • Elliptic partial differential equations: biharmonic equations and higher-order elliptic operators.
  • Unique continuation: quantitative unique continuation and estimates for the order of vanishing.
  • Geometric analysis: the size and local structure of nodal sets of solutions to elliptic equations.

My current interests center on unique continuation and nodal geometry for higher-order elliptic equations, particularly those involving the biharmonic operator Δ2. I am interested in how quantitative estimates for the order of vanishing inform the size and local structure of the nodal set.
